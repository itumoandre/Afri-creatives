Our ingredients

Rooted in heritage, selected for nourishment.

Hibiscus

Softening botanical blend

Known for its rich botanical profile and silky, conditioning feel in deep hair treatments.

Moringa

Lightweight nourishment

A silky oil choice for hydration, shine, and manageability, especially on dry ends and scalp.

Shea butter

Moisture seal

Helps soften dry hair and smooth textured strands for easier styling and a softer feel.

Baobab

Softening care

Rich in emollient properties for a softer feel and smoother-looking hair.

Aloe vera

Hydration boost

Commonly used in scalp and hair formulas for a freshening moisture balance and silky finish.
